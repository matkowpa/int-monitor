What I learned:

The freshest development is from 18 September, when Intrum announced it has entered into an agreement to co-invest in and become servicer to a Nordic non-performing loan (NPL) portfolio ([Inderes](https://www.inderes.se/en/releases/intrum-to-co-invest-and-become-servicer-to-nordic-npl-portfolio)). The release describes a co-investment structure in which Intrum both takes an economic stake and wins the servicing mandate — a signal, in my reading, that the company is returning to selective NPL deployment now that its balance-sheet repair has progressed, though the counterparty's name is truncated in the available text so I can't confirm who the partner is.

Earlier in the month, on 8 September, Intrum announced the results of tender offers for outstanding senior secured exchange notes of Intrum Investments and Financing AB ([Inderes](https://www.inderes.dk/en/releases/intrum-announces-the-results-of-the-tender-offers-for-outstanding-senior-secured-exchange-notes-of-intrum-investments-and-financing-ab-3)) — another step in ongoing liability management, though the snippet does not disclose the outcome figures.

These moves sit on top of a substantially repaired balance sheet. The Q2 2026 interim report, published 28 August, states that key balance-sheet strengthening initiatives were successfully executed and completed in July: a SEK 7.5bn fully guaranteed capital raise and a SEK 2.4bn portfolio sale, which contributed to rating upgrades by S&P (to B-) and Moody's (to B3). The same report notes operational transformation continuing with costs developing according to plan ([interim report PDF](https://storage.mfn.se/94ec9243-ed46-4a6f-8c67-3b20873b582a/interim-report-second-quarter-2026-intrum.pdf); [Inderes Q2 release](https://www.inderes.se/en/releases/intrum-strengthens-financial-position-in-second-quarter-with-successful-capital-raise)). S&P's own research update of 17 June confirms the upgrade to 'B-' on capital-related grounds ([S&P Global Ratings](https://www.spglobal.com/ratings/en/regulatory/article/-/view/type/HTML/id/3582408)).

The arc began in May: Q1 2026 was the first quarter of the new strategy, with overall financial development "according to plan," cost reductions ahead of plan but servicing income slightly behind, both compounded by FX, and the servicing leverage ratio roughly unchanged quarter-on-quarter, supported by consolidation of the JV Savoy Gro… ([MarketScreener](https://www.marketscreener.com/news/intrum-iab-q1-report-2026-eng-ce7f58d3d988fe27)). The same day, Intrum announced the ~SEK 7.5bn capital raise explicitly to accelerate deleveraging ([Intrum press release](https://www.intrum.com/press/press-releases/press-release-article/?id=7b9715a7-0214-404f-8567-bce3baac39c8)). All of this executes the "Intrum 2030" strategy and new financial targets presented on 29 January ([Intrum IR](https://www.intrum.com/investors/financial-reports-presentations/)); current S&P and Moody's ratings are tracked on the company's IR site ([credit rating page](https://www.intrum.com/investors/debt-credit-rating/credit-rating/)).
