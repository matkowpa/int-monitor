The phrasing implies the company had been aiming to hold servicing income roughly flat for 2026 and is now signalling that target is at risk — that reading is interpretation on my part, but it follows directly from the reported language.

The drivers named in the report are worth separating: "portfolio deterioration" points to problems on the investment side (the value of acquired debt books eroding faster than expected), while "slower sales" suggests weaker monetisation, and both fed into the Q2 income decline. The guidance caution, by contrast, is specifically about the servicing business — the fee-based income stream — which signals management sees market softness persisting into the rest of the year rather than treating Q2 as a one-off.
