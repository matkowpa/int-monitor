What I learned: Intrum's most recent disclosed move is a regulatory press release dated 18 September announcing an agreement to co-invest in and become servicer to a Nordic NPL portfolio ([Inderes](https://www.inderes.se/en/releases/intrum-to-co-invest-and-become-servicer-to-nordic-npl-portfolio)). The release describes a structure in which Intrum both takes a co-investment stake and assumes the servicing mandate; read against the company's strategy, that signals a push to grow fee-based servicing income while sharing — rather than fully carrying — the capital exposure, though the snippet does not name the counterparty or the portfolio size, so the deal's scale remains unconfirmed. A week earlier, on 8 September, Intrum announced the results of tender offers for outstanding senior secured exchange notes of Intrum Investments and Financing AB ([Inderes](https://www.inderes.se/en/releases/intrum-announces-the-results-of-the-tender-offers-for-outstanding-senior-secured-exchange-notes-of-intrum-investments-and-financing-ab-3)), extending this year's liability-management work.

The balance-sheet repair arc is the clearer through-line. On 7 May Intrum announced a fully guaranteed capital raise of approximately SEK 7.5 billion alongside its Q1 results, explicitly intended to accelerate deleveraging ([Intrum press release](https://www.intrum.com/press/press-releases/press-release-article/?id=7b9715a7-0214-404f-8567-bce3baac39c8)); the investor-relations page frames the raise as reducing financial risk and enabling delivery of the "Intrum 2030" strategy and new financial targets presented on 29 January ([IR page](https://www.intrum.com/investors/), [reports page](https://www.intrum.com/investors/financial-reports-presentations/)). The Q2 interim report states that key balance-sheet strengthening initiatives were successfully executed and completed in July ([interim report PDF](https://storage.mfn.se/94ec9243-ed46-4a6f-8c67-3b20873b582a/interim-report-second-quarter-2026-intrum.pdf)). Rating agencies responded: after shareholders approved the raise, Moody's upgraded Intrum's credit rating ([Inderes, 12 June](https://www.inderes.fi/en/releases/intrum-credit-rating-upgraded-by-moodys-following-shareholder-approval-of-capital-raise)), and S&P Global Ratings upgraded the issuer rating to B- with a positive outlook while also raising the rating on the 1.5-lien notes ([S&P release PDF](https://storage.mfn.se/2b072e5f-6f0f-45c1-825f-4e89ba702a3e/intrum-credit-rating-upgraded-by-sandp-global-ratings-with-positive-outlook.pdf)). Intrum's own credit-rating and maturity-profile pages — the latter updated as of 14 September 2026 — present these ratings and the debt schedule as the foundation for the business ([credit rating](https://www.intrum.com/investors/debt-credit-rating/credit-rating/), [maturity profile](https://www.intrum.com/investors/debt-credit-rating/maturity-profile/)).

Operationally, however, Q2 was soft. Investing.com reported on 28 August that servicing income fell and that weaker-than-expected developments in some markets — portfolio deterioration and slower sales — make it more challenging to keep servicing income broadly flat in 2026 ([Investing.com](https://ca.investing.com/news/earnings/intrum-flags-tougher-2026-servicing-outlook-as-q2-income-falls-4820872)). The interim report corroborates: costs are developing according to plan and servicing income is growing in Traditional markets, but not enough to offset the decline in Specialised markets ([interim report PDF](https://storage.mfn.se/94ec9243-ed46-4a6f-8c67-3b20873b582a/interim-report-second-quarter-2026-intrum.pdf)). Net reading: the balance sheet has been materially de-risked and re-rated, while the servicing P&L is the open watch item — and the September Nordic NPL co-investment looks like the first visible attempt to restart growth within it.
