Intrum's investor-facing materials point to a company executing a defined turnaround plan rather than generating fresh headlines. The anchor is the "Intrum 2030" strategy: Intrum's financial reports page states that on 29 January the company presented the result of a strategic review setting its direction for the coming years, including new financial targets, with a stated short-term strategy focus ([Financial reports & presentations](https://www.intrum.com/investors/financial-reports-presentations/)). The investor relations page treats Intrum 2030 as the reference point for the company's current capital markets activity ([Investor relations](https://www.intrum.com/investors/)).

The most substantive recent corporate action is a fully guaranteed capital raise of approximately SEK 7.5 billion. Per Intrum's press release, the raise was announced on 7 May 2026, with the terms of the offering published on 3 June 2026 ([Intrum announces terms of fully guaranteed SEK 7.5 billion capital raise](https://www.intrum.com/press/press-releases/press-release-article/?id=edbff921-0aed-4c13-9fea-7d49153e1153)). The investor relations page describes the stated purpose as accelerating Intrum's deleveraging path, thereby reducing financial risk and increasing the company's financial strength and flexibility to deliver on Intrum 2030 ([Investor relations](https://www.intrum.com/investors/)). Read as evidence, this signals that management judged balance-sheet repair a precondition for the strategy — deleveraging, not growth, is the near-term priority.

On the debt side, Intrum keeps its disclosures current: the maturity profile page was updated as of 24 July 2026 and provides an overview of the structure and timing of debt obligations, alongside a financing policy under which Group Treasury aims to secure a diversified funding base ([Maturity profile](https://www.intrum.com/investors/debt-credit-rating/maturity-profile/)). The credit rating page notes current ratings from S&P and Moody's and frames an external rating as a necessity for supporting the business ([Credit rating](https://www.intrum.com/investors/debt-credit-rating/credit-rating/)) — relevant context for the deleveraging goal, though the snippets do not disclose the actual rating levels.
